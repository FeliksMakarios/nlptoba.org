## Timeline

| Date | News |
|---|---|
| December 2026 *(planned)* | Guideline revised to version 2.0, research article, and final report of Stage 2. |
| November 2026 *(planned)* | Double annotation of the 250 pilot sentences completed, followed by the inter-annotator agreement analysis. |
| 11 November 2026 | Site update: complete English version, language-specific search, paginated pilot sentence explorer, and interactive dependency trees. |
| 7 November 2026 | Final [sentence-level parallel corpus](korpus/paralel.md) released: 1,542 pairs, 182 human-validated, with an inter-annotator agreement report. |
| 31 October 2026 | The [batak-toba-corpus-v2](https://github.com/FeliksMakarios/batak-toba-corpus-v2) repository made public. |
| 28 October 2026 | [Annotation guideline](anotasi-ud/pedoman.md) version 1.2: numbered glossary and term index, with no change to labelling rules. |
| 27 October 2026 | Guideline version 1.1: rules for compound and complex sentences and for *laos*, based on annotator feedback. |
| 5 October 2026 | Double annotation of the [250 pilot sentences](anotasi-ud/kalimat-pilot.md) started with guideline version 1.0. |
| 7 September 2026 | Alignment audit of the working parallel corpus (1,505 units), the source of the pilot sentences. |
| July 2026 | Stage 2 research, Universal Dependencies annotation, started. |
| 2025 | [Batak Toba corpus, Stage 1](korpus/index.md) completed as an output of the UPH Beginning Lecturer Research Scheme. |
