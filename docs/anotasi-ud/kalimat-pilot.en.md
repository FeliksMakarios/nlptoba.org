# 250 Pilot Sentences

These sentences are double annotated by two native Batak Toba annotators following [guideline v1.0](pedoman.md). They were selected from the alignment-revised corpus v1 (1,505 units) with these criteria:

1. Only 1-1 aligned units that passed routine review.
2. One complete sentence per unit, 5 to 20 words.
3. Stratified by genre and sub-genre, with a cap on sentences per document.
4. Priority for sentences containing the guideline's core constructions, such as *di-/hu-/ta-* verbs, the particles *do/ma*, negation, and the marker *na*.

!!! note "About the data"
    The Indonesian translations come from the Stage 1 corpus and have not yet been revalidated by speakers. Construction tags are computed automatically from spelling patterns and may be wrong.
